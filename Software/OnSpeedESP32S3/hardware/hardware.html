Pin map

GPIOSignalPeripheralWhy this pin

Left alone on purpose: 0, 3, 45, 46 (boot strapping), 35–37 (octal PSRAM), 19/20 (USB console), 43/44 (USB-UART bridge and ROM boot log), 38/48 (on-board LED). GPIO 11–20 are on ADC2, which Wi-Fi shares, so they carry only digital signals. The product PCB should use a WROOM-1-N8, never a "V" module.

Flap sensor

All three options use the same input (DB-15 pin 7). The firmware learns each detent in a ground calibration, switches stage with hysteresis and a 0.2 s debounce, and holds the last good stage if the sensor fails open or short.

Noise and reliability

Each item below is a problem found in the datasheets, Espressif's docs or issue trackers, and what the build or firmware does about it.

RiskReal numbersWhat is done about it
Pressure sensor noiseMS4525DO: 1.05 Pa per count, about 1.5 counts RMS. One P45 count is 0.085° of AOA at 50 kt.Median-of-15 then average-of-10 filter, as Gen2. Unit tests: steady AOA error 0.038°.
Bad or stale sensor readsStatus bits 10 = stale, 11 = fault. Gen2 ignored them.Stale and faulty samples rejected. 25 bad reads in a row mutes the tones instead of giving a wrong tone; it recovers on its own.
I²C hangESP-IDF issue 12929: the bus can lock with a stuck device.2 ms timeouts and a bus reset after 10 failures. External 4.7k pull-ups (the internal 45k is too weak).
I²C speedGen2 ran at 3 MHz; the S3 tops out at 400 kHz. A 4-byte read takes 117.5 µs.All reads for one 20 ms cycle take under 1 ms of bus time.
ADC noiseAbout 3 LSB RMS, 6 with Wi-Fi on, spikes near 30 LSB. Reference varies 1.0–1.2 V by chip.ADC1 only, curve-fitted calibration, 32-sample average, 100 nF at the pin. Flap detents sit hundreds of counts apart.
Audio clockThe PCM5102A PLL cannot lock to 22.05 kHz.I²S at 44.1 kHz.
Audio levelDAC 2.1 Vrms full scale; audio panel music inputs want under 0.2 Vrms at full volume.−12 dB pad plus a firmware cap. Set it on the ground with the audio panel.
Pops at power-upXSMT ramp 104 samples; the PLL needs 16 LRCK periods.XSMT held low by a pull-down until I²S is clocking zeros.
Power transientsBus can drop below 7 V when cranking; hot-plug spikes can pass 50 V.D36V28F5 runs from 5.3 V, TVS + PTC, 47 µF bulk, brownout detector on.
Wi-FiRaises ADC noise and costs CPU.Off in flight. Only on in ground maintenance mode (panel button held at power-up) or over USB.
Ground loopsGen2 shares power and audio ground on the board.Audio ground only at the audio panel; shielded twisted pair.
CPU headroomCoreMark: S3 1329.92, Teensy 3.6 440.72.About 3× the Teensy, with the second core free for the AHRS or display.

Bench tests before flight

These can only be checked on real hardware. None of them should be skipped.

  • Measure flap ADC noise in mV at each detent, with and without Wi-Fi. If detents are not cleanly separated, fit the ADS1115.
  • Check the audio level and listen for hum on the actual audio panel, engine running.
  • Power-cycle and crank the engine with the box on: no reset, no pop.
  • Measure the boom and display logic levels on each aircraft before connecting (3.3 V only).
  • Pull a sensor connector in flight-like conditions on the bench and confirm the tones mute and come back.
  • Redo the AOA calibration flight: the 5 V-calibrated MS4525DO parts run at 3.3 V, as in Gen2. New builds can buy the 3.3 V version (DS3).
  • Ask the FlyOnSpeed team (team@flyonspeed.org) about their ESP32 V4B board before laying out a PCB.
